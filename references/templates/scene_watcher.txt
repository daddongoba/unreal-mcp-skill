# Template: Existing-Scene Watcher (zero-touch attach: tag targets + sense box + settle detect + proximity touch)
# Verified: 2026-08-28 UE5.8 EN (user PIE-confirmed: cube sank on proximity touch; watcher touched nothing existing)
# Context: add behavior to an actor in a MADE scene (whitebox or production) WITHOUT editing that actor,
#          its blueprint, or any pre-existing asset. See few_shots case #8 for the workflow frame.

--- Concept ---
BP_Watcher (invisible logic actor, folder TestT_N) placed by the user (or agent) near the target:
  sense box latches the target by TAG (never by registration order), settle detector waits out
  physics falling, then PROXIMITY (not displacement) = touch. All effects act on TargetActor only.

--- Components (BP_Watcher) ---
ActorTools.add_component("/Script/Engine.BoxComponent", "SenseBox"):
  boxExtent sized to cover EXACTLY ONE target (multi-target = ambiguous arming);
  bGenerateOverlapEvents=true; bodyInstance.collisionProfileName="Trigger" (verify readback QueryOnly)

--- Target tagging (do this FIRST — green on agent-created actors; ask on user's) ---
ActorTools.add_tag(actor=<target>, tag="WatchTarget"); verify ActorTools.has_tag

--- vars ---
bArmed, bSettled, bFiring (bool); TargetActor (Actor object var); StartLoc, LastLoc (Vector); StillFrames, CurZ (float)

--- DSL: arm on tagged overlap only (#51: unfiltered = the PLAYER becomes the target) ---
(event AddEvent|Collision|EventActorBeginOverlap (OtherActor)
  (if (not (Variables|Default|GetArmed))
    (bind ok (Actor|ActorHasTag :self OtherActor :Tag "WatchTarget"))
    (if ok
      (Variables|Default|SetTargetActor OtherActor)
      (bind loc0 (Transformation|GetActorLocation :self OtherActor))
      (Variables|Default|SetStartLoc loc0)
      (Variables|Default|SetCurZ (.z loc0))
      (Variables|Default|SetArmed true)
      (Development|PrintString :InString "armed: tagged target"))))

--- DSL: settle detector + PROXIMITY touch (#52: flying pawn can't push; displacement never fires) ---
(event EventTick (DeltaSeconds)
  (bind tgt (Variables|Default|GetTargetActor))
  (if (Variables|Default|GetArmed)
    ... delta-vs-LastLoc dm2 ...
    (if (not (Variables|Default|GetSettled))   ; wait until STILL for ~0.5s (30 ticks)
      (if (< dm2 1.0) (SetStillFrames +1) (if (> StillFrames 30) SetStartLoc/SetSettled/Print"ARMED-at rest"))
      (SetLastLoc cur))
    (if (and Settled (not Firing))
      (bind pawn (Game|GetPlayerPawn :PlayerIndex 0))
      (bind pd (Transformation|GetDistanceTo :self pawn :OtherActor tgt))
      (if (< pd 250.0)  ...fire: GetComponentbyClass/SetSimulatePhysics(false)/SetFiring/Print... ))
    (if Firing ... effect on TargetActor only, e.g. FInterpTo sink 400 ...)))

--- Steps ---
1. add_tag on intended target(s) → 2. build BP_Watcher (component+vars+DSL) → compile → save
3. user places/moves the watcher (respect their placement; re-check coverage math after moves:
   zone ± boxExtent must wrap exactly ONE target) → 4. user PIE: armed→at-rest→approach→effect.
5. Re-verify coverage AFTER any user move (transforms change silently; #25/#53 landmines).
