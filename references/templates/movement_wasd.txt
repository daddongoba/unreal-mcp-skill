# Template: WASD Movement (variable speed)
# Verified: 2026-08-22, UE5.8, English locale, PIE-tested
# Requires: MoveSpeed (float, instance editable) — set 500.0 on placed instance
# MCP calls: add_variable, set_variable_instance_editable, write_graph_dsl, compile_blueprint

--- DSL ---

(event EventTick (DeltaSeconds)
  (bind dt (* DeltaSeconds (Variables|Default|GetMoveSpeed)))
  (bind pc (Game|GetPlayerController :PlayerIndex 0))
  (bind wDown (Game|Player|IsInputKeyDown :self pc :Key "W"))
  (bind sDown (Game|Player|IsInputKeyDown :self pc :Key "S"))
  (bind aDown (Game|Player|IsInputKeyDown :self pc :Key "A"))
  (bind dDown (Game|Player|IsInputKeyDown :self pc :Key "D"))
  (bind moveX (* (- (select dDown 1.0 0.0) (select aDown 1.0 0.0)) dt))
  (bind moveY (* (- (select wDown 1.0 0.0) (select sDown 1.0 0.0)) dt))
  (bind moveVec (Math|Vector|MakeVector :X moveX :Y moveY :Z 0.0))
  (Transformation|AddActorWorldOffset :DeltaLocation moveVec))

--- Steps ---

1. BlueprintTools.add_variable
   {"blueprint":"<BP_REF>","name":"MoveSpeed","type_name":"float"}
2. BlueprintTools.set_variable_instance_editable
   {"blueprint":"<BP_REF>","variable_name":"MoveSpeed","instance_editable":true}
3. BlueprintTools.write_graph_dsl
   {"graph":"<GRAPH_REF>","code":"<DSL above>"}
4. BlueprintTools.compile_blueprint
   {"blueprint":"<BP_REF>"}
5. SceneTools.add_to_scene_from_asset — place instance, set MoveSpeed=500 in Details

--- Verify ---

PIE: hold W → actor moves +Y; S → -Y; A → -X; D → +X. Speed scales with MoveSpeed.
Notes: binds per direction avoid double-execution (rule R10). bool→float via select (R4).
